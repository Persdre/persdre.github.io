<p class="thesis">I build LLMs that read financial text and turn it into investment decisions, and I study the biases that bend their judgment.</p>

<h2>Research</h2>

<ol class="pipeline">
  <li class="stage">
    <span class="stage-num">1</span>
    <h3>Data</h3>
    <p>Benchmarks the field is missing, built from the documents investors actually read.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29286">CrossAlpha</a>, EMNLP 2026 Findings. Do annual reports from one market predict returns in another?</li>
      <li><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=juE0rWGCJW">EX-Graph</a>, ICLR 2024. The first public dataset linking Ethereum wallets to X accounts. <a href="https://github.com/Persdre/EX-Graph">Code</a></li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li class="stage">
    <span class="stage-num">2</span>
    <h3>Decisions</h3>
    <p>Agents that reason over market information and trade on it.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.63.pdf">CryptoTrade</a>, EMNLP 2024. A reflective agent fusing on-chain and off-chain signals. <a href="https://github.com/Xtra-Computing/CryptoTrade">Code</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12464">Fact-Subjectivity Aware Reasoning</a>, ICLR 2025 Financial AI Workshop. Separating facts from opinions makes trading decisions better. <a href="https://github.com/Persdre/FS-ReasoningAgent">Code</a></li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li class="stage">
    <span class="stage-num">3</span>
    <h3>Judgment</h3>
    <p>Making sure the model's judgment follows the evidence and ignores the framing.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09946">Judging Bias in Large Reasoning Models</a>, COLM 2025. Reasoning models still fall for bandwagon, authority and position cues.</li>
      <li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01528">Treat Bias as Noise</a>, NeurIPS 2026. RL training that keeps reasoning robust on biased prompts.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ol>

<p class="also">I also work on multi-agent systems (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09955">MegaAgent</a>, ACL 2025 Findings, coordinating up to 590 agents) and on tracing model lineage (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24496">LLM DNA</a>, ICLR 2026 <span class="honor">Oral</span>). All 21 papers are on the <a href="/publications/">publications page</a>.</p>
